  questions:
    - question: "Is Pillsner a medical device?"
      answer:
        - "No. Pillsner is a reminder and a record, not a medical device and not a source of medical advice. It does not check doses, interactions or anything else a pharmacist or doctor would check."
        - "Never rely on it as your only reminder either: a phone can be switched off, silent or out of battery. Pillsner says so in the disclaimer it shows before you add your first medicine."
    - question: "Does Pillsner work offline?"
      answer:
        - "Always. Pillsner declares no internet permission at all, so it works exactly the same in flight mode as it does at home. Nothing it does needs a connection."
    - question: "Does it cost anything, and are there ads?"
      answer:
        - "Pillsner is free, has no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases. It is open source, and the code is on GitHub for anyone to read."
    - question: "Do I need an account?"
      answer:
        - "No. There is no sign-up, no profile and no password. You install the app and start adding medicines."
    - question: "Will the reminder arrive if my phone is asleep?"
      answer:
        - "Yes. Pillsner sets its reminders as real, exact alarms, which Android delivers even while the phone is dozing. That is also why a scheduled medicine puts an alarm icon in your status bar."
        - "If a dose ever comes due and no reminder arrived, the home screen tells you and points you at the background settings that caused it."
    - question: "What happens when I get a new phone?"
      answer:
        - "Pillsner stores everything on the device, so it does not carry your data across by itself. If Android's own backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include Pillsner's data in the backup it restores onto your new phone."
        - "The app lock is deliberately excluded from that backup and from device-to-device transfer, so you set it up again on the new phone."
    - question: "Can I delete a medicine?"
      answer:
        - "A medicine is never deleted; stopping one deactivates it. The medicine, its schedules and every dose it produced stay on your device, so your history keeps making sense."
        - "If you want everything gone, Settings has a Reset app button that erases every medicine, schedule and dose permanently."
    - question: "What is the difference between a skipped dose and a missed one?"
      answer:
        - "A skipped dose is one you deliberately answered with Not going to. A missed dose is one you never answered, which becomes missed when the next dose is due or 24 hours later, whichever comes first."
    - question: "Does Pillsner track stock or remind me to order a repeat prescription?"
      answer:
        - "Not today. Pillsner reminds you to take your medicines and records what you took. Stock and refill reminders are not part of the app."
    - question: "Which languages does Pillsner speak?"
      answer:
        - "English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese. The app follows your phone's language on its own, and you can override it in Settings."
    - question: "Do I need a Wear OS watch?"
      answer:
        - "No. The watch app is optional. Without a watch, everything works from the phone; with one, you also see the doses coming up in the next six hours on your wrist."
    - question: "How long does Pillsner keep my history?"
      answer:
        - "A rolling year. Doses older than a year are removed automatically; your medicines and their schedules are never touched by that."
