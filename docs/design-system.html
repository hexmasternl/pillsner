Design system · confirmed 11 September 2026

Pillsner

Your partner in taking your pills. Green for done, blue for due, red only when something needs you. Body set in Montserrat 18, headers in Raleway 48 ExtraLight.

check_circle Confirmed

This page mirrors docs/design-system.md in the repository. The document holds the full token tables and rules; this page shows what they look like. The page follows your own device theme, except the paired panels, which are pinned to light and dark so both can be judged side by side.

Principles

Calm by default, loud only when it mattersGreens and blues carry the app. Red is reserved for missed and overdue doses, undeliverable reminders and empty stock. Because red is rare, it is unmistakable.
Legible at 2 a.m.18 sp body, 48 dp targets, a 56 dp confirm button, AA contrast everywhere, and layouts that survive 200 % font scale by wrapping.
One tap to confirmEvery surface has one filled green action in the bottom third of the screen. Everything else steps back.
The system's theme is the app's themeLight and dark follow the OS. Both are designed, not inverted.
Brand colour, not wallpaper colourMaterial You dynamic colour is off. Pillsner is always green, blue and white.

Typography

Two anchors: Montserrat 18 / 400 for everything you read, Raleway 48 / 200 for the one header a screen gets. The rest of the Material 3 scale is derived from those. Raleway stays at or above 24 sp and steps up to weight 300 under 34 sp, because ExtraLight strokes lose contrast when small. Fonts are bundled in res/font, not downloaded.

Wordmark

The name blends Pills and Partner, and the wordmark shows it: Pills in secondary, ner in primary, in both themes. One word, no gap, one type role, one weight — a colour split, not two words.

Pillsner
Light · displayLarge
Pillsner
Dark · displayLarge

It is one text node built from a single annotated string, so a screen reader, text selection and a test matcher all see the whole word “Pillsner”, never Pills and ner separately. The colouring is decoration and carries no meaning. It stays at displayLarge or, at smallest, headlineSmall, because Raleway 200 is never used under 24 sp. It lives once in ui/components/PillsnerWordmark.kt and is derived from the app_title string resource.

Use errorContainer for overdue and missedUse red to make a heading "stand out"
Read colours from MaterialTheme.colorSchemeWrite a hex literal in a screen

Let text wrap at large font sizesTruncate anything but the app bar title
